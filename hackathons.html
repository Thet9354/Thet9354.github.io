Create4Good
SUTD x ESSEC Create4Good Hackathon
SUTD Venture, Innovation & Entrepreneurship x ESSEC Business School · 10–12 March 2023
Built HearAll — an Android accessibility app using AI sign language recognition to break down communication barriers for hearing-impaired users. Presented to a full auditorium on the final day; user validation showed 87.5% of respondents found it useful and 75% found it accessible to people unfamiliar with technology (8 hard-of-hearing respondents surveyed).
"Create4Good was the first time I built something with a real social purpose and had to defend it in front of people who would push back. We validated HearAll with actual hearing-impaired users, got the data, and presented it on a stage. That experience — building fast, validating with real users, then presenting the numbers — shaped how I approach every product since."
